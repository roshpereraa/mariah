The Song

A twenty-five-year run-up

🎵

Written in a hurry

Mariah Carey and Walter Afanasieff put the song together for Merry Christmas, her fourth studio album, released in October 1994. It was built to sound like a 1960s Christmas record rather than a 1990s one — sleigh bells, wall-of-sound piano, the lot.

📈

Number one, eventually

It took until December 2019 for the song to finally top the Billboard Hot 100 — twenty-five years after release, and Mariah's nineteenth chart-topper. It has returned to the summit every festive season since.

🎄

A seasonal institution

It soundtracks the end of Love Actually, every shopping centre from November onwards, and one very famous annual announcement that it is officially time. The Queen of Christmas does not take the crown off.

About

How this thing works

🔊

The music

The player tries three sources in order: a licensed copy of the record if one is present, then the official upload via YouTube's embed player, and finally an original sleigh-bell mix generated live in your browser. Something always plays.

🦦

The cursor

That is a hand-drawn reindeer following your pointer, complete with a glowing red nose, a happier face while you hold a click, and a trail of magic dust behind it.

❄

The snow

A few hundred flakes on a canvas, with parallax depth and a wind that wanders. It respects your reduced-motion setting, as does everything else that moves here.
